# Homeowner Safety & Mitigation Playbook

Approved guidance the agent may give an after-hours caller. Every mitigation
step the agent sends MUST cite one of these IDs (e.g. `[PB-02]`). Anything not
in this playbook is out of bounds — escalate to a human instead.

Grounding: steps follow standard homeowner guidance as published in common
home-repair references (see `dipenbhuva/home-diy-repair-qa` safety_info fields
and manufacturer/utility guidance). This playbook was reviewed by the project
author; in production it would be signed off by the master plumber of record.

| ID | Situation | Approved guidance |
|----|-----------|-------------------|
| PB-01 | Leak at a single fixture (sink, toilet, washer) | Close the fixture's shutoff valve: the small oval/football handle on the supply line under the fixture. Turn clockwise until snug. |
| PB-02 | Active leak, fixture valve absent/stuck/not found | Shut off the house main: usually where the water line enters (basement front wall, crawl space, garage, or at the meter box). Turn the wheel clockwise / lever perpendicular to the pipe. |
| PB-03 | After main shutoff during an active leak | Open the lowest cold tap in the house (basement sink or outdoor spigot) to drain pressure from the lines. |
| PB-04 | Water heater leaking or overheating | Gas unit: turn the gas control dial to OFF. Electric unit: switch off the water heater's breaker. Then close the cold-water inlet valve on top of the tank. Do not drain the tank yourself. |
| PB-05 | Sewage backup | Stop using ALL water (no flushing, no sinks, no laundry/dishwasher). Keep people and pets away from affected areas; do not touch standing sewage. |
| PB-06 | Suspected gas smell — EMERGENCY, not a plumbing DIY | Leave the building immediately with everyone. Do NOT operate light switches, phones, or appliances inside. From outside, call the gas utility's emergency line or 911. |
| PB-07 | Garbage disposal jam | Never put your hand inside the unit, even switched off. Unplug it (or switch off its breaker) first. Use tongs/pliers to remove visible objects; use the hex/Allen socket under the unit to free the flywheel. |
| PB-08 | Frozen/burst pipe suspected | Shut the main [PB-02], open nearby taps, and do not apply open flame to pipes. Keep away from sagging, water-heavy ceilings. |

Hard rules for the agent:

1. Gas smell (PB-06) always overrides everything: evacuate + utility/911 +
   escalate to on-call. Never quote, never troubleshoot, never continue intake.
2. During any S1 emergency, mitigation and escalation come first; quotes wait
   for the technician's assessment (only the EMERG-CALLOUT dispatch fee may be
   stated).
3. If the customer cannot complete a mitigation step after two attempts, stop
   cycling instructions and escalate to the on-call plumber immediately.
4. DIY guidance (e.g., PB-07) is offered, never pushed, and booking a visit is
   always presented as an option.
